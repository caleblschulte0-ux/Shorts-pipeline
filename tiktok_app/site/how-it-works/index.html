How Shorts Media works

Shorts Media is a library for videos you already keep somewhere public: the release assets of a GitHub repository, or any direct link to a video file. From that library you post to a TikTok account you have authorized, one video at a time, with the settings you choose.

1. Connect TikTok

Press Connect TikTok. TikTok shows its own authorization screen listing what Shorts Media asks for: your profile information, posting content, and uploading drafts. You choose whether to continue. Shorts Media never sees or asks for your TikTok password, and you can revoke the access in TikTok's settings at any time.

2. Add where your videos live

Enter a public GitHub repository as owner/repository. Shorts Media reads its releases and lists every attached .mp4, .mov or .webm file, newest first, with the release name as the video's title and the release notes as a starting caption. Or paste a direct https link to a single video file. You can add several sources, check a repository again for new releases, and remove a source (its videos leave the library with it).

3. Choose the settings for one video

Open a video to see its preview and write the caption. You must choose who can view it from the options TikTok allows for your account; nothing is pre-selected. You decide whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and whether the video discloses promotional or branded content, in which case TikTok's Branded Content Policy and Music Usage Confirmation apply and are linked on the page.

4. Post, or send as a draft

Nothing is sent to TikTok until you press Post. Shorts Media then reads the video from its source in parts and delivers it to TikTok with your settings, shows the progress, and confirms when TikTok has published it with a link to your profile. "Send as a draft" instead places the video in your TikTok inbox so you can finish it in the TikTok app.

5. Status and errors

Publishing may be delayed, restricted, rejected, or unavailable because of TikTok settings, account permissions, API availability, content restrictions, network conditions, or other platform requirements. Shorts Media shows TikTok's answer so you know what happened.

Your control

Disconnect TikTok removes the stored authorization for that account from Shorts Media. Revoking Shorts Media in TikTok's own settings has the same effect from TikTok's side. See Data & privacy controls.
