User controls

Data & privacy controls

This page explains the practical ways to control a Shorts Media connection and submit privacy-related requests.

Revoke TikTok authorization

Users can revoke an application's TikTok authorization using the controls TikTok provides for connected apps. Once access is revoked or expires, Shorts Media will not use that authorization for future access to the account.

Request access, correction, or deletion

Use the Shorts Media support form and select the appropriate privacy request type. To protect account information, Shorts Media may need to verify that the requester is authorized to make the request before acting on it.

What to include

  • an email address where a response can be sent;
  • the type of privacy request;
  • enough information to identify the relevant Shorts Media interaction or connected account without sending a password or other unnecessary secret.
Do not send passwords, client secrets, access tokens, or other authentication secrets through the support form.

Limits

Some information may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute handling, or other legitimate operational purposes. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and the rights of other people.
