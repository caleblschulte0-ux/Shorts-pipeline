About Shorts Media

Shorts Media is a web application focused on simplifying the workflow around preparing and publishing short-form video content while keeping account authorization and content responsibility clear.

Purpose

Shorts Media is designed to reduce unnecessary steps between prepared short-form video content and supported publishing destinations. Where a third-party platform integration is used, the integration depends on the user's authorization and the platform's own developer rules.

Principles

Use platform-provided authorization rather than requesting a user's platform password.
Be clear about what data is processed and why.
Give users a clear way to seek support and exercise privacy controls.
Require users to have rights and permission for the content they publish.

Third-party services

TikTok and other third-party platforms are separate services with their own rules, privacy practices, and availability. References to TikTok describe the integration and do not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation.
