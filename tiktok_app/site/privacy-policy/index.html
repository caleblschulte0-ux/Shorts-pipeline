Shorts Media Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026  •  Website: https://shorts-media.netlify.app/

This Privacy Policy explains how Shorts Media may collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when a person uses the Shorts Media website, support features, account authorization, and supported publishing functionality.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to Shorts Media's public website and to information processed by Shorts Media in connection with user-authorized account integrations and publishing functionality. Third-party services, including TikTok, operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features a user chooses to use, Shorts Media may process:

  • Account and authorization information: identifiers and account information made available through an authorized third-party integration, together with authorization status and the permissions granted by the user.
  • Authorization credentials: access or refresh credentials made available through the platform authorization process where needed to provide an authorized integration. Shorts Media does not ask users to provide their TikTok password directly to Shorts Media.
  • Library information: the sources a user adds (a public GitHub repository name, or a direct video link) and the list of videos found there (file name or release title, release notes, size, link and date). This is stored for the connected TikTok account so the library is there the next time the user returns. Shorts Media does not store copies of the videos themselves; a video file is read from its source only when the user posts it, and only to deliver it to TikTok.
  • Content and post information: the video the user chooses to post, its caption, privacy or interaction settings, disclosure choices, and other information a user selects or provides for supported publishing functionality.
  • Operational information: publishing status, timestamps, error messages, request identifiers, and other technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Support information: name, email address, request type, and message if a person chooses to contact Shorts Media through the support form.

3. How information is used

Shorts Media may use information to:

  • authenticate and maintain user-authorized account connections;
  • provide content preparation and supported publishing functionality;
  • transmit user-selected content and post information to an authorized third-party platform;
  • show status information and troubleshoot failed or incomplete actions;
  • respond to support, privacy, access, correction, or deletion requests;
  • protect the service, users, connected accounts, and platform integrations from misuse or security threats;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce the Terms of Service.

4. How information is shared

Shorts Media does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, including:

  • TikTok: when a user authorizes TikTok access or requests supported TikTok publishing functionality.
  • GitHub: when a user adds a GitHub repository, Shorts Media asks GitHub's public API for that repository's releases. Only the repository name is sent; GitHub receives the request under its own privacy policy.
  • Infrastructure providers: service providers used for website hosting, network delivery, storage, security, or support handling where they process information on behalf of the service.
  • Legal and safety purposes: where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or respond to lawful process.
  • Business changes: if the service or its assets are reorganized, transferred, or acquired, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

5. TikTok integration

When a user chooses to connect a TikTok account, TikTok controls its own authorization interface and determines the information and permissions it makes available. Shorts Media processes TikTok-related information only to provide the features authorized and requested by the user, subject to TikTok's developer requirements and service availability.

TikTok is a third-party service and is not controlled by Shorts Media. TikTok's own privacy policy and terms govern TikTok's processing of information.

6. Retention

Shorts Media seeks to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including maintaining an authorized connection, completing requested publishing operations, resolving support or security issues, meeting legal obligations, and maintaining appropriate operational records. Authorization credentials that expire or are revoked are no longer used to access the connected account.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, the user's actions, technical requirements, and applicable law.

7. User choices and privacy controls

Users may have the following controls, depending on the information and applicable law:

  • Revoke TikTok authorization: revoke an app's access using TikTok's account controls.
  • Request access or information: ask what information Shorts Media has associated with the requester's account or support interaction.
  • Request correction or deletion: request correction or deletion of information where applicable and subject to legitimate legal, security, or operational retention needs.
  • Ask a privacy question: use the Shorts Media support form.

Instructions and a direct request path are available at https://shorts-media.netlify.app/data-and-privacy/.

8. Security

Shorts Media uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No website, network, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Website hosting, cookies, and logs

The public Shorts Media website is hosted on Netlify. The website itself does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts in this release. Hosting and network providers may generate ordinary technical logs or use essential technologies needed to deliver, secure, and operate the site. If a visitor submits the support form, the submitted information is processed through Netlify's form-handling infrastructure for delivery to the site operator.

10. Children

Shorts Media is not directed to children who are below the age at which they may lawfully use the relevant services or provide the required consent in their jurisdiction. Users must also satisfy the eligibility requirements of any connected third-party platform.

11. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements, or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised Policy is published.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, deletion requests, authorization issues, or general support, use the Shorts Media support page:

https://shorts-media.netlify.app/support/
