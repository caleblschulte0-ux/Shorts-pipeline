SFX in this directory (whoosh.wav, boom.wav, riser.wav, pop.wav) are
procedurally synthesized by make_sfx.py (numpy). They are original works
authored for this project and released into the public domain (CC0 1.0).
No third-party samples are used, so there is no licensing obligation on
uploads that include them.

vine_boom.wav is NOT synthesised: it is the "Vine boom" meme sound effect,
fetched from a public soundboard (myinstants.com/media/sounds/vine-boom.mp3)
by scripts/fetch_sfx.py on 2026-10-11 at the operator's request, trimmed
and loudness-normalised. It is used as a brief emphasis hit, at most once
per clip (third_capture/moves.py MAX_WHOA).
