## Viewer-facing additions

- Adaptive caption typography with dynamic font sizing, numeric emphasis, contrast
  enforcement, orphan-word prevention, platform UI safe zones, and collision-free
  placement around the hero visual.
- Sound-design planning with early hook impacts, number-synchronized ticks, bounded
  cue density, voice ducking, Shorts-safe loudness targets, and a silence window
  immediately before consequence/payoff hits.
- Transition continuity through object matches, number matches, data morphs,
  callback returns, impact cuts, and repetition-aware fallback selection.
- A measurable tension curve that keeps the hook energetic, escalates through the
  explanation, peaks on the consequence, and releases on the payoff.
- Dedicated visual-mechanic families for five common channel archetypes:
  money over time, share of whole, ranking, two-value comparison, and single stat.
- Deterministic 1080×1920 SVG hook and payoff frames plus complete JSON bundles for
  every archetype.
- A perceptual critic covering typography, audio synchronization, transition
  continuity, tension, useful occupancy, archetype fit, and callback payoff.
