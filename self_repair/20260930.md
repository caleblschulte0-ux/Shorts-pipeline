# Self-repair 2026-09-30: a source credit is read at full size, whole (trending graph_race + explainer)
**Class:** the on-screen source line is "tiny grey, cut off at '...McDonald's US'", "'(ITU...' truncated", "microscopic". 21 verdicts on 7 stories (dollar_general ×4, student_debt ×2, podcast, butter, global-ewaste, rural-power, landline), 19 of them inside an `unreadable` auto-fail. It was the only auto-fail on dollar_general (83) and student_debt (74), which were today's 2 blocked graph races. The headless judge came back after its weekly limit today. On 09-28/29 the verdicts were gemini-fallback and named none of this.
**Cause:** three earlier fixes (09-11, 09-24, 09-25) fitted the line, and a fitter can only shrink and then cut. The defect was LENGTH. `credit_line` kept 96 characters and missed a sentence end after ")". The explainer drew the full provenance: publisher, dataset and access date, median 83 characters and up to 318. The race fitter also measured the regular-weight credit in bold.
**Fixed:** `credit_line` ends a sentence after a bracket and before a dash. It trims at a seam (",", " and ", " / ") that keeps the publisher, within 64 characters, which is what 18pt holds. It measures the weight it draws. `charts._footer` draws `Source.credit()`, the publisher only. `footer()` keeps full provenance for the description and QA. Across 700 real datasets, shrunk-or-cut explainer footers fall from 399 (170 with "…") to 6, and those 6 are paragraph-length publishers. Re-rendered dollar_general, student_debt and e-waste all show the credit whole at full size.
**Test:** `tests/test_a_source_credit_is_read_at_full_size.py`. On old code 7 of its 8 tests fail. All 8 pass on the new code, and so do the 52 modules touching chart_race/charts/sources.base (results in the PR).
