# Self-repair 2026-09-29: a full-frame picture is measured at the length it plays (explainer)
**Class:** explainer beats that hold still. Yesterday's gauge/timeline fix (#484) did not hold. `temporal_gate` blocked coldest-place 6 times on 09-28/29 (0.525-0.534 against 0.45), 21 times since 09-07. `dead_air` hit beaver, iss, hidden-foster and global-ewaste. That is 10 auto-fails on 5 stories. Reddit `junk_imagery` (4) comes from media supply, and `decorative_mascot` on gameplay comes from the rubric (protected).
**Cause:** every full-frame picture (brain mechanic, kit scene, diorama) is paced by `reveal`, and nothing measured it at the span it was given. `mechanic_motion_ok` checks one frame pair at each of 5 points of a 7s beat, and `render_procedural` then wrapped the reveal in its own `1-(1-r)**2`. Offline, the flyout passed the probe and played 0.88 held over its 13.8s span (36-48s of the master). Seg0's scene played 0.72 held over 10.5s.
**Fixed:** `render_procedural` uses `settle()`. `charts.render_story_build` measures each full-frame span with the reviewer's own detector against the active phase's ceiling. A frozen span steps down the FALLBACK chain, but it is kept (never traded for another still) unless something further down actually moves. `studio_render` persists a mechanic only if it was what was BUILT.
**Test:** `tests/test_a_mechanic_is_measured_at_the_length_it_plays.py`: 10 tests. On old code it fails on the defect itself (a frozen span plays at 1.0, the flyout at 0.89, the ease-out tail at 0.97). All 866 tests in the modules touching the build path pass, and so do the 49 viz_scene modules once cairo is installed.
**NOT FIXED — coldest-place is still blocked.** Re-rendered with the fix, nothing in either chain moves at that length: diorama 0.82/0.97, and bubbles also frozen (a single 2.94e16 bubble clipped into a slab). So the original plays and the master is unchanged. The next step is the SPAN, not the picture. seg2's 13.8s comes from `cover_every_window` stretching a neighbour over a failed window. Either split that window, or give the mechanic sandbox a frame-paced clock (as `charts._flow` did for the gauge). The render log now names the frozen span (`[chart] ... refused: 0.88 ...`), which scene_repair could not ("no scene identified anywhere").
**Cost:** a refused span renders its fallbacks too, so a video with frozen spans renders slower.
